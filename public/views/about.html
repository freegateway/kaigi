About Kaigi

Kaigi is the FreeGateway Project’s self-hosted video conferencing system for FreeBSD. It is developed independently as a fork of MiroTalk SFU and uses mediasoup for WebRTC media.

Kaigi puts privacy, authenticated meeting access and administrator control first. Browser resources are hosted locally, without third-party tracking or translation services.
