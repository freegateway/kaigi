Kaigi privacy information

This page describes Kaigi software. The organization operating this installation is responsible for its privacy policy, contact details, logging and retention rules. Ask that organization for the policy that applies to your meetings.

Accounts and meeting access

Every participant signs in and may join only authorized meetings. Kaigi uses a secure session cookie and browser storage for authentication, meeting selection and preferences. Authentication and operational logs can contain connection and account information.

Media and recordings

The conferencing server handles meeting media. Transport encryption does not provide end-to-end encryption against that server. Recording availability and server uploads depend on administrator settings and meeting restrictions. Participants can also use external recording tools.

Local resources and administrator choices

Kaigi serves its supported browser assets and translations locally and excludes built-in cloud integrations and analytics. Administrator-configured services, site customizations and links opened by users may contact other systems. Log and recording retention depend on the deployment.
